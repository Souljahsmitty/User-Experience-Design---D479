Travel Info & Contact

Currency, Safety & Transport

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Safety is top-notch, with a low crime rate. Transport options include affordable taxis, bike rentals, and walking-friendly streets.

Map & Directions

Arriving is easy! Flights arrive at Taniti International Airport. From there, taxis or shuttles can take you to resorts and main attractions within minutes.
